DNA lab (for the DNA bells voice)
Paste A, C, G, T letters (U is read as T; anything else is dropped). The starting sequences are invented examples, not real genes.
Graph: gold line is the share of G and C in a sliding 9-base window; dots are each base (A C G T) as a step height; shaded bands are the parts found; the white line is where the bells are now.
What is the pair strand?
DNA is two strands wound together. A always pairs with T, and C always pairs with G, so each strand is a copy-recipe for the other. The partner strand runs the opposite direction: we write the top strand 5' to 3' and the pair strand under it 3' to 5'. To read the pair strand in its own 5' to 3' direction you reverse it (the second copy button does that). Either strand can carry a gene. In the audio, the pair strand plays each base's partner as a quieter harmony note.
What the parts mean, and what this tool cannot tell you
Parts are found by simple rules: a start codon (ATG), codons read in threes using the standard genetic code, and a stop codon (TAA, TAG, TGA). Real genes also have switches, introns that get cut out, and untranslated ends that letters alone cannot show. To find out what a real sequence actually is, paste it into NCBI BLAST. The pitch mapping (A first step, C second, G third, T fourth; or amino acid to step) is a choice we made, not biology. Nothing here claims that music changes DNA.
How it fits together
Frequency: every cell shows the actual Hz it plays. Change the Root frequency, the Tuning, or "Shift ALL" and every track retunes together. The Bar 1 to 4 ratios move the whole band bar by bar.
Clips and scenes: each track holds four clips (A to D). A scene picks one clip per track and turns tracks on or off. Tap a scene to switch at the next bar. Song order chains scenes for a set number of bars.
Equation link: the equation sets how a voice changes over its cycle: heat smooths the tone, Burgers turns it to a buzz as the slope blows up, the wavepacket widens, and so on. Guitar, trombone, bass and hum voices follow the equation you pick for them.
Lights: each track draws its own equation curve as a bundle of fibers, colored by track, mixed additively, split into a prism fringe by loudness. 8D swirl orbits each track around you (use headphones) and the lights follow the same orbit.
Record video saves the lights and the sound together. This is a sonification and an art piece: it can point you at patterns, it does not prove anything.
