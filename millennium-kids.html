Seven questions worth $1,000,000 each

In the year 2000, the Clay Mathematics Institute picked seven math questions nobody has answered and offered $1,000,000 for each correct solution. It announced the list on May 24, 2000, at the Collège de France in Paris. One has been solved so far (number 3, Poincaré), and its solver turned the prize down.
